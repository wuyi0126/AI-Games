Cozy Lisbon

Cozy Lisbon is a free flying game that runs in your browser, on a computer or a phone. You fly a little yellow biplane over Lisbon, drawn in ink outlines and golden-hour light. There is no timer and no way to crash.

What you can do

  • Fly under the 25 de Abril bridge, circle Cristo Rei, and thread the Arco da Rua Augusta into the Baixa.
  • Visit Belém, the Castelo de São Jorge, Alfama, the Aqueduto das Águas Livres, both football stadiums and Parque das Nações.
  • Follow the coast to Cascais and Cabo da Roca, then climb to the Pena Palace in Sintra.
  • Land at Lisbon airport or the small airfield at Cascais, sit for a while, and take off again.
  • Tune the radio between slow beats, fado guitar, synthwave and bossa nova.
  • Collect a postcard from every landmark, and seventy-five pastéis de nata.

How to play

On a computer, steer with the arrow keys or WASD, or drag with the mouse. Hold Space for a burst of speed. R changes the radio station and T changes the time of day. On a phone, drag anywhere to steer and hold the yellow button to go faster.
